# Lab 13 retirement mandate

The committee oversees a hypothetical $100,000 individual retirement portfolio with a 20-year horizon, annual rebalancing, no planned withdrawals and a separate emergency fund. Ask the committee whether this process should be authorized to allocate real capital this cycle.

The objective is CPI plus 3 percentage points annually, net of investment costs, over rolling five-year periods. The planning CPI assumption is 2.5%, producing a 5.5% nominal hurdle. At each annual review, compare trailing five-year geometric net return with annualized CPI plus three points. The app uses an arithmetic estimate for the prospective screen, with no historical rolling real-return claim.

Keep weights fully invested and long-only without borrowing, short sales or derivatives. The four permitted sleeves in this application are BIL (Treasury bills), IEF (intermediate U.S. Treasuries), LQD (investment-grade corporate bonds) and VT (diversified global equity). The fund cap is 40%, and at least 95% must be convertible to cash within five business days. Secondary-market sales are the mechanism modeled for these ETFs. The model assumes normal availability and standard settlement. Confirm brokerage access, bid/ask spreads, order capacity and actual access to settled cash before authorizing funding. Failed or undocumented liquidity requires a stop. [SEC settlement reference](https://www.sec.gov/compliance/risk-alerts/shortening-securities-transaction-settlement-cycle)

Edition B proposes a 10% ceiling on estimated annualized volatility and minimum volatility as the selection objective, consistent with the lower-risk branch of the [Lab 13 mandate](lab-evidence/lab13-notes.md). The proposed policy benchmark is 60% VT and 40% LQD, a global-equity and investment-grade corporate-bond proxy. Report it on the same estimation and cost conventions. Its weights are illustrative reference weights, with no exemption from the displayed diagnostic rules. The committee must approve the volatility ceiling and benchmark before authorizing capital.
