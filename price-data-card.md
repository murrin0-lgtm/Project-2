# Price data card

The model reads [prices.csv](../data/prices.csv). The four normalized daily adjusted-close captures and capture metadata are in [data](../data), with ticker-specific source links in [prices_sources.json](../data/prices_sources.json). The capture timestamp is October 22, 2026 at 19:16:53 UTC. Coverage starts September 2021 and ends September 2026, with 61 month-end prices and 60 monthly returns. SHA-256 `dfdceaafc55363d15b40e70c5c2e91bfa14271a904009948f57a4d6cce326762`.

Use the final trading observation of each completed month, and calculate simple returns as current adjusted close divided by prior adjusted close minus one. Require four positive, finite prices per month, strictly consecutive months and the stated ticker order. Exclude partial October and any nonprice inputs. No interpolation, winsorization or currency conversion is applied. All funds are USD-traded and the adjusted series are used as fund-level total-return proxies.

Annualize the monthly mean by multiplying by 12 and the sample covariance by multiplying by 12. Use the same estimation window for each portfolio. Adjusted prices embed fund expenses through the observed share-price path. Deduct 10 bp per year for incremental implementation friction, with 50 bp as the higher-cost stress. This is a scenario allowance for trading and operations, with taxes and actual trade-level prices excluded. CPI is a 2.5% planning assumption, and modeled liquidity is documented in the [mandate](mandate.md).

Yahoo adjusted-close histories can revise with corporate actions or vendor updates. The local capture supports replay as acquired, and its capture date limits claims about historical availability.
