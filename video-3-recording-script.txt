VIDEO 3 — INVESTMENT COMMITTEE PRESENTATION

Read the SAY blocks aloud. Use SHOW and DO as screen cues. Allow about
two to three minutes of narration plus screen changes. Check the assignment cap.

BEFORE RECORDING

Open the local Streamlit app and start the recording with it already running.
Reset the sidebar to defaults, including Planning CPI at 2.5% and no funds
selected as unavailable. Open docs/locked-changed-input-prediction.md in a
separate tab. Rehearse raising only Planning CPI to 3.5%. Confirm the header
above Portfolio comparison shows a 6.50% net hurdle, the Constrained row
shows “STOP: no feasible mix,” and the warning shows 5.93%. Reset CPI to
2.5% before recording. Read the ownership statement only after reviewing the
analysis and deciding you stand behind the recommendation.

1. OPENING AND COMMITTEE ACTION

SHOW — Start on “Retirement Portfolio Review” with the red decision banner
and the default sidebar in view.

SAY
“I used OpenAI Codex to help build this app, run checks, and draft the
committee memo. I reviewed the analysis and take responsibility for the
decision. This project is for education and does not constitute investment
advice. My recommendation is to authorize zero dollars in new live funding
for this process, an allocation range of zero to zero percent. I recommend
paper monitoring this cycle.”

2. WHY THE BASE CASE ISN’T ENOUGH

SHOW — Point to the Constrained row in “Portfolio comparison.” Then point
to the 5.50% hurdle in the caption above the table. Scroll to “Required
stress runs” and point to the VT estimate stress.

SAY
“Under the base assumptions, the constrained mix is forty percent BIL,
twenty percent IEF, zero percent LQD, and forty percent VT. Its estimated
net return is five point seven four percent, and estimated annual volatility
is seven point zero eight percent. It clears the net hurdle by about
twenty-four basis points. Cutting the estimated VT return by two percentage
points lowers the return ceiling to five point one three percent. That stress
is enough to stop funding.”

3. PREDICTION AND CHANGED-INPUT DEMO

SHOW — Open the saved prediction and point to the planned CPI change and
predicted outcome. Return to the app.

SAY
“Before running this changed case, I recorded a prediction. Raising planning
CPI from two point five to three point five percent would raise the net
hurdle to six point five percent. I predicted a stop because the best
estimated return under the other rules was five point nine three percent.”

DO — In “Scenario controls,” change only “Planning CPI (%)” to 3.5.
Wait for the app to refresh. Show the new hurdle, the Constrained row’s stop,
and the warning below the comparison tables. Keep the sidebar visible.

SAY
“The app now shows a six point five percent hurdle and a five point nine
three percent ceiling. That leaves a gap of about fifty-seven basis points.
Changing CPI raised the required return while the estimated returns, cost,
and other rules stayed the same. The result matches the prediction and
reinforces my zero-dollar recommendation.”

4. WHEN TO REOPEN THE DECISION

SHOW — Scroll to “Archived interest-rate signal.” Point to the 5.29%
reading and the duration review message.

SAY
“I would reopen the decision after the desk verifies five-day access to at
least ninety-five percent of the portfolio, the committee approves the
benchmark and risk ceiling, and an independently supported forecast clears
the net hurdle under the required stresses. The archived yield of five
point two nine percent also crossed the five percent duration review
threshold. I would complete that review before voting on funding again.”
