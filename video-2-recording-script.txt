2. FROM PRICES TO ESTIMATES

SHOW — Open data/prices.csv. Point to the date and four ticker columns.
Switch to python/portfolio.py and search for def estimate_from_csv.
Scroll to the line starting with returns = and the estimates below it.

SAY
“The application starts with monthly adjusted closes for BIL, IEF, LQD, and
VT. The parser checks that months are consecutive and prices are positive.
Each monthly return is the current price divided by the previous price,
minus one. The function annualizes the average returns and sample
volatilities and calculates the correlations used in the covariance matrix.”

3. FROM ESTIMATES TO WEIGHTS AND VERDICTS

SHOW — Open python/decision.py and find def evaluate. Point to the target,
net, and rules lines, then to the candidate loop and the update to best.

SAY
“The decision function adds three percentage points to planning CPI to get
the net return hurdle. It subtracts the annual cost from gross return
and checks each candidate for full investment, long-only weights, the fund
cap, five-day liquidity, volatility, and net return. It searches in five
percentage point increments and picks the lowest-volatility mix that passes
every rule. It also tracks the highest net return among mixes that pass
the other rules. That number helps explain a stop.”

SHOW — Point to the returned portfolios in decision.py. Switch to the
running app and show the two tables under “Portfolio comparison.”

SAY
“The app shows the result alongside equal weight, inverse volatility, and
the sixty-forty benchmark. The first table shows the weights and estimates.
The second shows the pass or fail result for each rule.”
